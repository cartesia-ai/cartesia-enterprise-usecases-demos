# Expense Reimbursement

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable, business-related expenses. Submit them through the finance portal within 30 days, with an itemized receipt for anything over $25.

Standard limits: meals up to $50 per day while travelling, economy airfare, and standard hotel rooms. Any expense over $500 needs manager approval before it is incurred.

Approved reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle.

Not reimbursable: personal expenses, alcohol beyond a modest amount with a client meal, and travel upgrades.
