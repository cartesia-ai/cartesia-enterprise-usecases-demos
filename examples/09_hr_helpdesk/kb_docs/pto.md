# Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year, earned monthly. PTO covers vacation, personal days, and illness.

Request planned time off through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. For unexpected illness, notify your manager as soon as you can.

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year; any balance above that is forfeited on December 31. Accrued, unused PTO is paid out when you leave the company.

Parental leave is separate from PTO: 12 weeks of paid parental leave is available to all full-time employees after 6 months of employment.
