# Health and Retirement Benefits

The company offers medical, dental, and vision coverage to all full-time employees and their dependents. Coverage begins on the first day of the month after your start date.

Open enrollment runs each November. Outside that window, you can only change your elections after a qualifying life event — marriage, a new child, or loss of other coverage — and you must do so within 30 days of the event.

The company matches 401(k) contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 4% of your salary, and the match vests immediately.

Enroll or change your elections through the benefits portal.
