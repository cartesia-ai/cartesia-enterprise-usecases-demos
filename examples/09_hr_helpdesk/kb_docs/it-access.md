# IT Access and Equipment

New employees receive a laptop and standard software on their first day.

To request additional software, hardware, or access to an internal system, file a ticket in the IT portal. Most requests are approved within two business days.

VPN access is required for remote work. Install the company VPN client from the IT portal and sign in with your company credentials.

Forgot your password? Use the self-service reset link on the login page. If you are locked out, contact the IT helpdesk.

Report a lost or stolen device to IT immediately so it can be remotely secured.
